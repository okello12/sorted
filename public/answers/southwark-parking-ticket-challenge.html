Got a Southwark parking ticket? Keep the stage, deadline and evidence together.

A PCN can move through several stages. The safest starting point is the notice itself: who issued it, the PCN number, vehicle registration, amount, dates and the exact stage shown on the document.

What Southwark says

Southwark’s parking pages say a PCN can be paid or challenged. Later notices can create different response periods, and if a representation is rejected the council explains the route to an independent adjudicator.

Southwark Council: challenge a PCN ↗

Before submitting anything

  • Keep a photo or PDF of every notice, including the date and PCN number.
  • Keep photographs, permits, payment receipts or app records that relate to what happened.
  • Use the deadline printed on the actual notice if it differs from a date calculated elsewhere.
  • Keep the exact wording you submit and the confirmation or reference after submission.

How Sorted helps

Sorted reads the notice, proposes the key facts for you to confirm, keeps the deadline and evidence, helps prepare a factual challenge from confirmed information, links to the official route and then waits for the response without losing the thread.

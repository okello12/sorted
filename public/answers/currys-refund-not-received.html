Before you chase it

  • Check the account or card the refund was meant to go back to.
  • Keep the order number, refund amount and the date Currys said it would arrive.
  • Save any email, chat screenshot or reference number confirming the refund.
  • If Currys gave a time window, note exactly when it ended.

When you contact them

Ask one clear question: whether the refund has actually been processed, and if so, on what date and with what transaction or case reference. If it has not been processed, ask for a new date.

If the problem relates to faulty goods, Citizens Advice has an official plain-English guide to refund, repair and replacement rights.

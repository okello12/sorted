Adviser Mode

Spend the appointment advising, not reconstructing the story.

A Sorted user can prepare a case pack before an appointment: concise summary, chronology, promises, dates, evidence, disputed points and the questions they need answered.

Try Sorted as a consumer →

What the pilot is testing

Whether one real advice organisation can receive clearer cases with less time spent asking the user to rebuild a timeline from messages, screenshots and memory.

The first pilot is deliberately small. There is no organisation-wide dashboard yet. The user controls the case and chooses what to share.

What the adviser receives

Case summary · important deadlines · chronology · what the other side says · what the user says happened · evidence list · outstanding questions.

The pack is an organisation aid, not a legal conclusion. Sorted does not choose a plea, settlement, appeal strategy or other consequential decision.

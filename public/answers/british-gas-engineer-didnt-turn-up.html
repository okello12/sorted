Missed appointment

They gave you an engineer window. Nobody came.

Keep the original appointment, booking or job reference, the time window and any message about cancellation or delay. Then ask for the new appointment and a reference for the missed visit.

What to record

  • The date and full appointment window.
  • The job, booking or account reference.
  • Whether anyone cancelled, and when they told you.
  • Any costs or practical impact caused by the missed visit.
  • The new appointment or response date they give you.

Do not assume every missed engineer visit has the same compensation rule

British Gas appointments can relate to different products and services. A boiler or HomeCare visit is not the same thing as a fixed broadband appointment under Ofcom’s automatic compensation scheme. Check the terms and complaint route that apply to the service you actually have.
